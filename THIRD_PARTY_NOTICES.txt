This distribution contains software and generated hardware resources from third parties. Cargo.lock and gateware/uv.lock are the authoritative version inventories. Source is available from the repositories listed below or the registry identity recorded by the lockfile.

Embedded Glasgow resources
--------------------------

The embedded C0-C3 FPGA bitstreams and Cypress firmware are generated from Glasgow Interface Explorer software at commit:

  https://github.com/GlasgowEmbedded/glasgow/tree/b2a15e9c797b90167d96257a557218ddb7984e71

Glasgow software declares the license expression 0BSD OR Apache-2.0. Its copyright and license texts are available at that pinned source revision. The build environment also uses Amaranth and the YoWASP Yosys/nextpnr toolchain recorded in gateware/uv.lock. Those build tools are not shipped as runtime executables.
